Research

My work moves along two threads, both concerned with how conspiracy theories construct narratives that justify antidemocratic behavior.

Political violence

Under what conditions do conspiracy theories embolden support for political violence? This thread treats conspiracy theories as rhetorical frames, examining how their effects vary across contexts and interact with pre-existing political attitudes.

Conspiratorial environments

How do conspiracy narratives form, evolve, and travel between online and offline spaces? This thread traces why some narratives gain traction — and are co-opted by far-right movements — while others fade.

Methods — qualitative, quantitative, and computational.

Research interests — conspiracies, political violence, democracy, far-right movements, masculinity, American politics.
